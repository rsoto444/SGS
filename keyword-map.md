**Hard to beat for a new site**

**Most realistic to start with**

# How sure am I

- **Volumes** come from Google Ads data, which rounds small numbers into buckets. Treat them as a ranking of which categories matter, not exact counts.
- **Close variants** (for example "bakeries provo" and "bakeries provo utah") often show the same number because Google groups them. Do not add them up.
- **Near me searches** show higher numbers because Google counts them across a wider area. They are listed as secondary keywords, never as the primary.
- **Difficulty** is a national score. Local directory pages compete against Yelp, Google Maps and big directories, so this is practitioner guidance, not documented Google behaviour.
