# Referral checklist

Made Saturday 3 October 2026 · for every lead that says yes to sharing their details · you refer each one by hand to ONE listed business
**Next:** the next time a "User query" task shows up in GHL, work down this list.

## Before you start

- **A "User query" task or a "human handover" tag means someone said yes.** Their yes is in the text or call conversation, not in the contact fields.
- **No yes in the conversation, no referral.** If you can't find a clear yes, stop and ask them first.
- **Never give the person a business name or a time before you have confirmed it.** The bot only says "we'll pass your request on to a local business."

## The steps

1. **Open the conversation, not just the task.** The task only says "Yes". The real request is in the messages above it.
2. **Find the clear yes.** Look for their answer to "Is that okay? Reply YES or NO." Anything other than a clear yes means no referral.
3. **Write down the trade, the city and the problem** in the contact: Referral Category, Referral City and Referral Details. The bot does not fill these in, so you do.
4. **Pick ONE listed business** in that category. It must have a premium listing AND have ticked the "send me leads" box. No such business? Set Referral Status to **No business available** and stop.
5. **Send the lead to that one business** by phone or email, with only the name, number and request the person agreed to share. Nothing else.
6. **Set Referral Status to Referred.** Add a note with the date, the business you sent it to, and "yes found in conversation".
7. **Tell the person, once.** A short text: "We passed your request on to a local business. They may contact you by phone, text or email." Do not promise a time.
8. **Mark the task complete.**

## Special cases

- **They said no, or they are not sure.** Do not refer. The bot already offered to take a message. If they leave a message, answer it yourself.
- **They want a person, not a referral.** Reply yourself and mark the task complete.
- **They change their mind later.** Stop. Do not share anything further.
- **They text STOP.** GHL blocks further texts to them. Do not text them from any other number.

## Rules that never change

- One business per lead.
- Never name or recommend a business in a bot message.
- Never promise results, prices or timing.
- Sharing details without a clear yes is never okay.
